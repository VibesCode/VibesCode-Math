# Sources

- K. Ono, "Integer values of tan(arctan 1 + … + arctan n) are rare", arXiv:2607.05739 (v1, July 2026). Remark 3.3 lists the twelve indices 15, 17, 80, 82, 395, 397, 1904, 1906, 9163, 9165, 44086, 44088 where the paper's method gives no conclusion.
- V. H. Moll, Sci. Ser. A Math. Sci. 24 (2013) 90–119: a numerical scan for 5 ≤ n ≤ 50000 and a congruence result (Theorem 1.6, n ≡ 5, 13 mod 19).
- T. Amdeberhan, L. A. Medina, V. H. Moll, J. Number Theory 128 (2008): the conjecture.
- DeepMind formal-conjectures, `FormalConjectures/Arxiv/2607.05739/TanArctanSum.lean`, declaration `tan_arctan_sum_not_integer` (marked research open).
